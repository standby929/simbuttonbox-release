# Privacy information — draft, not final policy

SimButtonbox is designed to connect an Android device to a Windows companion over a local network. Pairing and device connection information are used to establish and maintain that connection. The Windows application can create local diagnostic logs when logging is enabled. Users should review and redact logs before sharing them for support.

The final privacy policy will be published before public Android distribution. It must identify the controller and contact details, describe actual data processing, retention, legal bases and user rights where applicable, and cover Google Play Billing once integrated. This draft does not assert that no data leaves the device and is not intended as a final Google Play privacy policy.
